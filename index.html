Core Services

Lawn care, planting and hardscape — one accountable crew

Six disciplines, one standard. Every visit is scheduled, every edge is cut clean, and every price is agreed before we start.

01

Weekly & Bi-Weekly Precision Lawn Maintenance

Mowing at the correct height for your turf, string-trimmed borders, blown-clean hardscape, and alternating stripe patterns that keep Houston lawns sharp all season.

02

Custom Landscape Design & Planting

Drawn layouts, considered plant selection for Houston heat and clay soil, and installation that matures well — from single focal beds to full front-yard rethinks.

03

Fresh Sod Installation & Turf Restoration

Grading, soil prep and premium St. Augustine or Zoysia sod laid tight, watered in, and handed over with a simple care schedule for the first three weeks.

04

Mulching, Bed Edging & Seasonal Cleanups

Hand-cut trench edges, the right mulch depth for Houston humidity, leaf removal, and bed refresh that makes an established yard read as maintained and intentional.

05

Tree & Shrub Trimming & Hedge Sculpting

Structural pruning, sightline clearing, and hedges squared to a string line — shape kept crisp without stripping the plant or leaving the bed littered.

06

Hardscaping, Pavers & Drainage Solutions

Paver patios, walkways and stoops set on proper base, plus French drains and grading that move Houston stormwater away from the slab instead of into the beds.
